# BA Case Management Software Development

## Overview
A state-level regulatory agency needs to replace its outdated software system for managing cases. The organization spans over 10 divisions, each with complex workflows. The old system lacked automatic communications and had a lot of manual steps that had to be enacted by a staff member of the organization. This project was a retrospective done based on a real-world technology solution for the business problem. I worked, alongside 2 other business analysts in training and 5 solution configurators in training, to gather and define requirements based off of artifacts, recordings, and documentation from the project's lead business analyst. Specifically, I referenced:

* One-on-One Stakeholder Interviews
* Workflow demonstrations
* JAR (Joint Application Requirements) sessions
* JAD (Joint Application Development) sessions
* Meeting Minutes

Because this project is retrospective and was designed for the solution configurators to implement in a month — the same time we had to gather and define requirements — the scope of the project was significantly reduced and many divisions were cut out of the workflows we produced. This demonstrates a capacity to make decisions about the scope of a project given strict constraints on time and resources. Additionally, the collaboration helped me hone my leadership and interpersonal skills. By scheduling meetings, organizing collaboration sessions with stakeholders, and ensuring that every requirement is met and traceable, we were able to align stakeholder interests and agree on a common goal for the project.

## Business Problem
The business problem is that the old system was outdated and inefficient, causing wasted time due to manual steps and lack of automatic communication. For example, service lists (lists which describe who notices need to be served to) were compiled manually by members of the Records staff division. When they wanted to delete an individual off a list, the system wasn't able to edit the list, instead it produced an entirely new list with everyone but the excluded party. These lists could not be automatically managed using the old system.

## My Contributions
* **Requirements Elicitation:** Derived business requirements from recordings of Joint Application Requirements (JAR) sessions, Joint Application Design (JAD) sessions, and meeting minutes.
* **Process Modeling & Improvement:** Designed as-is and to-be process flow diagrams to identify inefficiencies and drive future-state improvements.
* **Analysis:** Conducted fit-gap and stakeholder analyses to align solution capabilities with organizational needs.
* **Requirements Translation:** Converted business requirements into actionable functional, non-functional, and technical requirements.
* **Stakeholder Management:** Coordinated across the BA team to align priorities, manage deliverables, and maintain consistent documentation standards.
* **Documentation:** Produced comprehensive project artifacts to support traceability and stakeholder alignment.
